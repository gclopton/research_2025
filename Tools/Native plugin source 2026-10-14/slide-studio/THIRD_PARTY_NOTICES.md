# Illinois Block I

`assets/illinois-block-i.svg` is the unmodified official orange-and-white Block I from https://cdn.brand.illinois.edu/logos/block-i/orange-and-white.svg, retrieved 2026-09-28. It is bundled for offline presentation use. The university mark is not covered by the embedded editor’s MIT license. Brand reference: https://brand.illinois.edu/visual-identity/logo.

# Illinois formal wordmarks

`assets/illinois-formal-dark.svg` and `assets/illinois-formal-light.svg` are unmodified official formal wordmarks retrieved 2026-09-28 from https://cdn.brand.illinois.edu/wordmarks/formal/orange-and-white.svg and https://cdn.brand.illinois.edu/wordmarks/formal/orange-and-blue.svg. Bundled for offline presentation use. These university marks are not covered by the embedded editor's MIT license. Reference: https://web.brand.illinois.edu/logos/.

`assets/illinois-block-i-on-white.svg` is the unmodified official orange-and-blue Block I from https://cdn.brand.illinois.edu/logos/block-i/orange-and-blue.svg, retrieved 2026-09-28, for white-backed dark-mode banners. It is a university mark, not covered by the editor's MIT license.
