# Study Studio workspace

Question editors, question previews, exam builders, and exams open in Obsidian tabs. Switch between your notes and these tabs without losing the work in an open editor. Drag their tabs into a split to work beside a source note.

Use the **Move to new window** icon in the view header, or **Study Studio: Move current Study Studio tab to new window** in the command palette. You can maximize that window or use macOS's green window button for full screen.

**Ctrl+Enter** saves a question or exam builder draft. A preview has **Back to editing**. Authoring drafts stay in memory while their tab stays open: save before closing it or restarting Obsidian. Exam responses save automatically; finish any Math Studio equation and wait for **Exam saved locally**. Use **Study Studio: Resume exam or view exam results** after closing an exam or restarting.

Small selection dialogs and equation editors still use popups.
