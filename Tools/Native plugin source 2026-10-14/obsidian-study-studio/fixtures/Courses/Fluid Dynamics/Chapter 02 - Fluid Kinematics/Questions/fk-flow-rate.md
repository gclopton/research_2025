## Prompt

A stationary section carries $Q=0.12\ \mathrm{m^3/s}$ through area $A=0.03\ \mathrm{m^2}$. The mean normal speed is {{blank:a0}} $\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

$\bar u=Q/A=4\ \mathrm{m/s}$.
