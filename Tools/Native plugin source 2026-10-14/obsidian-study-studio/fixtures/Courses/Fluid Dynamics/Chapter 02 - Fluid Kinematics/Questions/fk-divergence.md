## Prompt

For $u=2x,\ v=-y,\ w=3z$ in SI units, $\nabla\cdot\mathbf V=$ {{blank:a0}} $\mathrm{s^{-1}}$. A tiny element of current volume $0.5\ \mathrm{m^3}$ has $D\mathcal V/Dt=$ {{blank:a1}} $\mathrm{m^3/s}$.

## Explanation

The trace is $2-1+3=4$; multiply by current volume to obtain 2.
