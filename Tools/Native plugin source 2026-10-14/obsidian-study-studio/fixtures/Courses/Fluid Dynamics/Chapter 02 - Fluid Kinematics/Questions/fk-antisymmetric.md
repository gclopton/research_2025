## Prompt

An antisymmetric $3\times3$ angular-velocity tensor has {{blank:a0}} independent components, and each diagonal entry is {{blank:a1}}.

## Explanation

$\omega_{ji}=-\omega_{ij}$ pairs the off-diagonal entries and forces $\omega_{ii}=0$.
