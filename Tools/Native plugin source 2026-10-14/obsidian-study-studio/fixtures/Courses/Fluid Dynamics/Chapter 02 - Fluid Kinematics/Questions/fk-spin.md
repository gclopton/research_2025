## Prompt

Using $\partial v/\partial x=4$ and $\partial u/\partial y=2$ in $\mathrm{s^{-1}}$, the spin about +z is {{blank:a0}} $\mathrm{s^{-1}}$ and the z-vorticity is {{blank:a1}} $\mathrm{s^{-1}}$.

## Explanation

Spin is $(4-2)/2=1$; vorticity is twice spin.
