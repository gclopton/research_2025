## Prompt

In nondimensional variables $f=x^2+t$ and $u=3$. At $x=2$, $\partial_t f=$ {{blank:a0}}, $u\partial_x f=$ {{blank:a1}}, and $Df/Dt=$ {{blank:a2}}. Enter numbers.

## Explanation

The local rate is 1; advection gives $3(2x)=12$; their sum is 13.
