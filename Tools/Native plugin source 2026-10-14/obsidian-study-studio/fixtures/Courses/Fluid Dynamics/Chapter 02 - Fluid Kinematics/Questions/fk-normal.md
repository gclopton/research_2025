## Prompt

Let $u=-3x$ with $x$ in metres and $u$ in metres per second. The normal strain rate is {{blank:a0}} $\mathrm{s^{-1}}$. The material line undergoes {{blank:a1}}.

## Explanation

$\partial_xu=-3\ \mathrm{s^{-1}}$, so lines along $x$ shorten.
