## Prompt

At a point $\partial v/\partial x=4$ and $\partial u/\partial y=2$, both in $\mathrm{s^{-1}}$. Using counterclockwise-positive rates for both lines, $r_x=$ {{blank:a0}} and $r_y=$ {{blank:a1}} $\mathrm{s^{-1}}$.

## Explanation

The +x line turns at $\partial_xv$, while the +y line has counterclockwise rate $-\partial_yu$.
