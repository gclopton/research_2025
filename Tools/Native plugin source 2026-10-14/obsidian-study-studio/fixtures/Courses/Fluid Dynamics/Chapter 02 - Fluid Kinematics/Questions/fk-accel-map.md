## Prompt

For $u=ax$ with constant $a=2\ \mathrm{s}^{-1}$, at $x=3\ \mathrm m$ the particle acceleration is {{blank:a0}} $\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Enter a number.

## Explanation

$Du/Dt=u\,\partial_xu=a^2x=12$. The local acceleration is zero.
