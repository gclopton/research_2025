## Prompt

For $u=-3y,\ v=3x,\ w=0$ in SI units, spin about z is {{blank:a0}}, engineering shear is {{blank:a1}}, and z-vorticity is {{blank:a2}}, all in $\mathrm{s^{-1}}$.

## Explanation

Difference of cross gradients is 6, sum is zero; spin is half the difference.
