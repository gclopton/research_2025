### 7.3 Graph Neural Networks and Message Passing — A

*Typical course titles:* Graph Neural Networks; Geometric Deep Learning; Deep Learning on Graphs.

## 7C. Interatomic Potentials

### 7.6 Machine-Learned Interatomic Potentials — A

*Typical course titles:* Machine Learning Interatomic Potentials; Atomistic Machine Learning; Machine Learning for Materials Modelling.

**Core content:**

- Energy decomposition; force learning through energy gradients; virial and stress prediction; conservative forces.
- Gaussian approximation potentials; kernel potentials; neural-network potentials; equivariant potentials; NequIP and Allegro concepts.
- Energy, force, and stress weighting.
- Model deployment in MD; computational cost and scaling; failure modes.

**Known leads:** NPTEL — materials-oriented machine-learning and computational-materials courses connecting materials physics, data generation, and ML.

**Core content:**

## Machine Learning Scope Guard

Do not spend most of the time on image classification, language models, or generic business datasets. After statistical-learning fundamentals, move quickly toward graph neural networks, equivariant learning, atomistic representations, MLIPs, active learning and uncertainty, and redox-aware and long-range models.
