# 3. Applied Mathematics

## 3A. Linear and Functional Analysis

### 3.1 Advanced Linear Algebra and Spectral Theory — A

*Typical course titles:* Advanced Linear Algebra; Matrix Analysis; Linear Algebra for Scientists and Engineers.

**Core content:**

- Vector spaces and dual spaces; inner-product spaces; orthogonal projections.
- Unitary and Hermitian operators; spectral theorem; generalized eigenvalue problems.
- Singular-value decomposition; positive-definite matrices; matrix functions.
- Tensor-product spaces; low-rank approximation.
- Conditioning at a conceptual level.

### 3.2 Functional Analysis and Operator Theory — B

*Typical course titles:* Functional Analysis; Hilbert Space Methods; Operator Theory for Quantum Mechanics.

**Core content (extract):**

- Normed and Banach spaces; Hilbert spaces; function spaces.
- Bounded and unbounded operators; domains of operators; self-adjointness; compact operators.
- Weak convergence; distributions.
- Projection operators; spectral decompositions; variational characterizations of eigenvalues.

**Omit:** abstract theorems with no connection to quantum operators, PDEs, or approximation.

## 3B. Variational and Symmetry Methods

### 3.3 Calculus of Variations and Variational Methods — A

*Typical course titles:* Calculus of Variations; Variational Methods; Variational Principles in Physics.

**Core content:**

- Functionals and functional derivatives; Euler–Lagrange equations; multiple fields.
- Constraints and Lagrange multipliers; boundary terms; natural boundary conditions.
- Second variation and stability; Rayleigh–Ritz method; weak formulations; gradient flows.
- Variational formulations of DFT, elasticity, diffusion, and phase field.

### 3.4 Group Theory, Representation Theory, and Tensor Operators — A/B

*Typical course titles:* Group Theory in Physics; Group Theory for Chemists; Representation Theory; Symmetry in Condensed Matter.

**Core content:**

- Groups, subgroups, classes, and cosets; representations and irreducible representations; character tables; direct products.
- Rotation group; spherical harmonics; angular-momentum coupling; Clebsch–Gordan coefficients; spherical tensors.
- Crystal point groups; space-group representations at a working level; symmetry-adapted basis functions; selection rules.
- Equivariance.

**Scope note:** supports f-orbital physics, crystallography, phonons, PAW multipoles, and equivariant neural networks — study alongside module 7.4 in [[07. Machine Learning for Atomistic Materials]].

## 3C. Transforms, Green's Functions, and PDEs

### 3.5 Fourier Analysis, Distributions, and Green's Functions — A

*Typical course titles:* Fourier Analysis; Integral Transforms; Green's Functions; Mathematical Methods for Physicists.

**Core content:**

- Fourier series and transforms; discrete Fourier transforms; convolution.
- Dirac delta and distributions; reciprocal-space representations; Laplace transforms.
- Green's functions for Poisson, diffusion, and wave equations; boundary-value Green's functions; response functions.
- Spectral methods; cylindrical transforms and Bessel functions where needed.

### 3.6 Partial Differential Equations — A

*Typical course titles:* Partial Differential Equations; Applied PDEs; Mathematical Methods for Diffusion and Waves.

**Core content:**

- Classification of PDEs; elliptic, parabolic, and hyperbolic equations; initial and boundary conditions.
- Heat and diffusion equations; wave equations; Poisson and Laplace equations.
- Separation of variables; eigenfunction expansions; maximum principles; conservation laws.
- Weak solutions at an introductory level; nonlinear diffusion; coupled PDE systems.
- Cylindrical and spherical geometries.

**Known leads:** NPTEL — introductory and advanced PDE courses, plus courses on numerical ODEs and PDEs.

## 3D. Probability and Asymptotics

### 3.7 Probability Theory and Stochastic Processes — A/B

*Typical course titles:* Graduate Probability; Stochastic Processes; Stochastic Methods in Physics.

**Core content:**

- Multivariate probability; conditional expectation; laws of large numbers and central-limit behavior.
- Markov chains; continuous-time Markov processes; master equations; Poisson processes; random walks.
- Langevin equations; Wiener processes; Fokker–Planck equations; first-passage processes.
- Correlation functions; Monte Carlo estimators.

## 3E. Dynamics and Optimization

### 3.9 Dynamical Systems, Stability, and Bifurcation Theory — B

*Typical course titles:* Nonlinear Dynamics; Dynamical Systems; Stability and Bifurcation Theory.

**Core content (extract):**

- Fixed points; linear stability; phase space; Lyapunov stability.
- Bifurcations; multiple attractors; metastability; gradient systems.
- Stiffness; pattern formation; reaction–diffusion instability.

**Scope note:** helps with SCF branches, phase-field dynamics, coupled transport, and nonlinear model behavior.
