# 4. Computational and Numerical Methods

## 4A. Numerical Foundations

### 4.1 Scientific Computing, Approximation, and Error Analysis — A

*Typical course titles:* Scientific Computing; Computational Science; Numerical Analysis.

**Core content:**

- Floating-point arithmetic; truncation and roundoff; conditioning versus stability; convergence.
- Interpolation; numerical differentiation; quadrature.
- Error propagation; algorithmic complexity.
- Reproducible numerical experiments; verification by manufactured or analytically solvable cases.

### 4.2 Numerical Linear Algebra and Eigenvalue Algorithms — A

*Typical course titles:* Numerical Linear Algebra; Matrix Computations; Large-Scale Eigenvalue Problems.

**Core content:**

- Power and inverse iteration; Lanczos and Arnoldi methods; generalized eigenproblems; subspace iteration.

### 4.5 Numerical PDEs — A

*Typical course titles:* Numerical Methods for PDEs; Finite Difference Methods; Finite Volume Methods; Finite Element Methods.

**Core content:**

- Consistency, stability, and convergence; spatial and temporal discretization; explicit and implicit schemes.
- Finite differences; finite volumes; finite elements; spectral methods.
- Boundary-condition implementation; nonlinear diffusion; coupled parabolic systems.
- Adaptive grids; conservation-preserving methods.
- Verification and mesh-convergence studies.

**Known leads:** NPTEL — graduate courses on finite-difference PDE methods and numerical solution of ODEs and PDEs.

## 4B. Atomistic and Electronic-Structure Algorithms

### 4.7 Molecular Simulation and Molecular-Dynamics Algorithms — A

*Typical course titles:* Molecular Simulation; Molecular Dynamics; Computational Materials Modelling.

**Core content:**

- Interatomic potentials; neighbor lists; periodic boundaries; minimum-image convention; long-range electrostatics.
- Thermostats; barostats; ensembles; stress tensors.
- Transport coefficients; nonequilibrium MD.
- Finite-size and finite-time effects; trajectory analysis; initialization and equilibration; reproducibility.

**Known leads:** NPTEL — *Foundations of Computational Materials Modelling* (crystal construction, statistical mechanics, molecular dynamics).

### 4.8 Algorithms for Plane-Wave DFT and PAW — A/B

*Typical course titles:* Computational Electronic Structure; First-Principles Materials Modelling; Electronic Structure Algorithms.
