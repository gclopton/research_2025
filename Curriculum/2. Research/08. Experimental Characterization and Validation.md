# 8. Experimental Characterization and Validation

*A supporting, interpretive track. The goal is not to become an instrument operator; it is to understand what an experiment actually observes and how directly it constrains the models.*

## 8A. Structure and Imaging

### 8.1 X-Ray Diffraction and Rietveld Analysis — A/B

*Typical course titles:* X-Ray Diffraction; Diffraction Methods; Crystallographic Characterization.

**Core content:**

- Scattering geometry; reciprocal-space interpretation; structure factors.
- Powder diffraction; peak positions and intensities; broadening; preferred orientation.
- Rietveld refinement; phase fractions; superstructure peaks.
- Limits of average-structure refinement; vacancy-ordering signatures; distinguishing phases with similar fluorite frameworks.

### 8.2 Total Scattering, Pair Distribution Functions, and Disorder — A/B

**Core content:**

- Bragg versus diffuse scattering; total scattering; pair distribution functions.

### 8.3 TEM, STEM, and Electron Diffraction — B

*Typical course titles:* Transmission Electron Microscopy; Electron Microscopy of Materials; XRD and TEM.

**Core content:**

- Electron scattering; diffraction contrast; selected-area diffraction.
- High-resolution TEM; STEM imaging; bright- and dark-field imaging.
- Sample thickness and projection; beam damage; spatial resolution.
- Track imaging; disordered oxygen versus cation sublattices; limits of interpreting projected images.

**Known leads:** NPTEL — courses covering XRD, optical microscopy, SEM, and TEM, including a dedicated XRD/TEM sequence.

## 8B. Spectroscopy

### 8.4 X-Ray Absorption, Photoelectron Spectroscopy, and EELS — A/B

*Treat as one broad spectroscopy module initially; split later if necessary.*

**Core content:**

- Core-level excitation; selection rules.
- XANES; EXAFS; XPS; EELS.
- Chemical shifts; multiplet and final-state effects; oxidation-state fingerprints; coordination sensitivity.
- Spatial versus spectral resolution; quantification of Ce³⁺/Ce⁴⁺; ambiguity between formal valence and fitted spectral components.

### 8.5 Vibrational Spectroscopy of Defects — B/C

*Typical course titles:* Raman Spectroscopy; Infrared Spectroscopy of Solids; Vibrational Spectroscopy of Materials.

**Core content (extract):**

- Normal-mode symmetry; Raman and infrared selection rules.
- Phonon confinement; disorder-induced modes; vacancy-associated features.
- Peak shifts and broadening; temperature effects.
- Connection to calculated phonon spectra; limitations of assigning individual peaks to defects.
