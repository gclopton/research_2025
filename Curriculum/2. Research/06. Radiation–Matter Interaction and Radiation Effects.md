# 6. Radiation–Matter Interaction and Radiation Effects

## 6A. Ion–Matter Interactions and Collisional Damage

### 6.1 Interaction of Radiation with Matter — A

*Typical course titles:* Interaction of Radiation with Matter; Radiation Physics; Charged-Particle Interactions.

**Core content:**

- Cross sections; mean free paths; energy-transfer kinematics.
- Elastic and inelastic collisions; ionization and excitation; energy-loss distributions.
- Electrons, ions, photons, and neutrons as distinct projectiles.
- Stopping versus scattering; secondary-particle production; relevant relativistic kinematics.

**Omit:** courses dominated by medical dosimetry or reactor-neutron calculations.
