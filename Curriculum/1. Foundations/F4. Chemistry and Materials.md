# F4. Chemistry and Materials

### F4.1 Physical Chemistry: Thermodynamics and Kinetics — Assess

**Why it's here:** The mass-action machinery that defect chemistry (research 2.5) reuses wholesale; single combined course per vault convention.

**Required content:**

- Chemical thermodynamics: activities, fugacity, equilibrium constants.
- Chemical potential in reacting systems.
- Rate laws, reaction order, activation energy, Arrhenius behavior.
- Catalysis and surface reactions at first exposure.

### F4.2 Quantum Chemistry and Molecular Structure — Assess

**Why it's here:** The bonding-intuition layer under DFT (1.2) and solid-state chemistry (2.2).

**Required content:**

- Molecular orbital theory; LCAO; bonding and antibonding.
- Hybridization; molecular geometry.
- Hartree–Fock ideas at first exposure.
- Spectroscopic consequences of electronic structure at a light level.

### F4.3 Inorganic Chemistry — Assess

**Why it's here:** Periodic trends, oxidation states, and coordination chemistry — the descriptive layer under research 2.2 and 2.3.

**Required content:**

- Periodic trends; ionic radii; electronegativity.
- Oxidation states and redox chemistry.
- Coordination chemistry; ligand-field ideas at first exposure.
- Introduction to extended solids and ionic lattices.

### F4.4 Introduction to Materials Science and Engineering — Assess

**Why it's here:** The Callister-level bridge into research area 02's graduate treatment.

**Required content:**

- Crystal structures and defects at survey level.
- Phase diagrams at survey level.
- Mechanical, thermal, and electrical properties overview.
- Processing–structure–property relationships.

**Note:** Survey course only: every topic here has a full graduate module in research area 02.
