# F6. Nuclear and Radiation

### F6.1 Introduction to Nuclear Science and Engineering — Assess

**Why it's here:** The undergraduate layer research area 06 assumes while deliberately excluding nuclear engineering as a degree.

**Required content:**

- Nuclear structure basics; binding energy; nuclear reactions at survey level.
- Radioactive decay; decay chains; activity.
- Radiation types and their interactions at survey level (the detailed treatment is research 6.1).
- Units and dosimetry language: dose, fluence, LET, cross sections.

### F6.2 Radiation Detection and Measurement — Assess

**Why it's here:** Knoll-level detection: how radiation is actually measured — the instrument-side language for irradiation experiments (research 6.12) that no research module teaches.

**Required content:**

- Detector physics: gas, scintillator, semiconductor detectors.
- Pulse processing and counting statistics.
- Spectroscopy systems; energy calibration and resolution.
- Dosimetry measurement practice at survey level.
